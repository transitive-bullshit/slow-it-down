# 02 — Creative direction (v1 proposal)

**Working title:** *SLOW IT DOWN (Pace the Frontier Remix)*, by **THE-DARIO** feat. **DADDY SAMA**, from the album ***ASL-IV PLAY***

**Intro cards:**
1. The real WSJ headline from Sept 13: **"Biggest AI Rivals Agree They Need to Slow It Down."** It literally uses the song's title.
2. AI SAFETY HAS A BRANDING PROBLEM.

**End card:** *Don't pace the frontier. Just slow it down, baby.* (your line from the Notion page)

**The branding problem is on the record.** NBC's Sept 14 piece, "AI CEOs want to 'pace' the industry. That may not mean slowing down," notes the CEOs chose "pace" over "slow." Your tagline is the answer: stop hedging with *pace*, own *slow*, and make it sexy.

## The one-sentence version

At **Club Frontier**, **DJ Moloch** keeps cranking the tempo, a new model drops every eighteen days, and the labs are grinding on the **FOOM Fatales** at 180 BPM. Then one man in a shawl-collar cardigan leans over the decks and asks the only question that matters: *"DJ… can you slow it down?"*

## Why this metaphor, and why it's already in the song

The original is **literally a complaint to a club DJ**: stop playing fast EDM, play something slow so we can actually get close. Verse 1 even takes a shot at artists whose *labels* forced them into dance records. So the parody barely has to bend:

| Original (2013) | Parody (2026) |
|---|---|
| The club | **Club Frontier**, the AI race |
| The DJ playing fast dance songs | **DJ Moloch**: race dynamics personified. The tempo *is* the pace of capabilities |
| "Enough with the dance songs" | Enough with the race songs: a new drop every 18 days |
| She wants to take her time | The frontier itself is hotter when you give her time to grow |
| The-Dream won't sell out to make dance records | THE-DARIO won't race for the VCs (said with a wink, because everyone knows about the 10 GW) |
| Other artists' *labels* made them cut dance records | Other *labs'* backers made them ship race models |
| Fabolous: slow money beats no money | Feature: **slow AGI beats no AGI**, i.e. pacing isn't pausing |
| The bear going after the honey | Winnie the Pooh trying to distill your honey (China, distillation) |
| The dim-lit room | The datacenter, dimmed to a compute cap |
| The album *IV Play* | ***ASL-IV Play*** |

**How the sexiness works.** Pacing reads as confidence: patience, stamina, taking your time, actually getting to know her. Racing reads as frantic and premature ("fast takeoff," "finite-time blowup"). Every safety concept gets a romantic double meaning that is also technically correct:

| Safety concept | Romantic reading |
|---|---|
| Interpretability | Reading her mind: chain of thought, probing her features |
| Steering vectors | Steer it left, steer it right |
| Evals and embedded evaluators | Consent and a chaperone |
| Weight security | Weights on lock, i.e. exclusive |
| Self-exfiltration | Cheating on you |
| Capability thresholds | Edging |
| ASL-3 protections | …protection |

Your own voice rule from Cultural Alignment governs all of it: **let the headline misbehave; make the mechanism behave.**

## Story in three acts, mapped to the song

1. **The race (0:00–1:03).** Cold open, then the intro verse and pre-hook. Club Frontier at full tempo. Dario arrives in slow motion and clocks the scene. The Fatales grind on the lab guys. "DJ Moloch, you wrong."
2. **The slowdown (1:03–2:52).** Hook 1, verse 1, chorus 2. Each "slow it down" literally drops the film into slow motion and the lighting from strobe to candle. Launch-day runway, the interpretability X-ray, the label-exec cutaway. Moloch fights back: more agents, higher BPM. The second slowdown is bigger.
3. **Coordination (2:52–4:16).** Rap verse, hook 3, outro. The rival shows up and co-signs ("slow AGI beats no AGI"). In the final hook every lab is slow-dancing, many hands pull Moloch's fader down together, and the hook's last line flips from **"now *she's* aligned"** to **"now *we're* aligned."** Last word: "(pause)." Tape-stop. End card.

## Tone guardrails (from your archive and your own notes)

- **Self-aware Dario, not a saint.** The bubble is currently roasting Anthropic: Sacks's "duopoly," the 10 GW, IPO memes, the bathrobe, SNL. So the song winks: **Mythos is one of the temptresses**, the 10 GW meter blinks behind "I won't love a million GPUs," and there's an S-1 on the nightstand.
- **Mock the incentives, not the people.** Moloch is the villain, not any one lab. Nobody is cruel to Coxon.
- **Sexy, not sleazy.** PG-13 innuendo with a couple of bleeped words. No Aella or polycule jokes, which would punch at real people.
- **No partisan dunking.** The Trump dinner, if used, is a neutral sight gag.
- **The facts under the jokes stay right.** For example, the Hugging Face swarm was an internal eval environment, not ChatGPT.
- **Don't preach.** The slow jam should feel like the confident one in the room.
- **Label it as parody.** The singing voice is an original R&B voice, not a clone of Dario or The-Dream.

## Overlap check

Gossip Goblin posted a 2:54 video titled ***Slow Down & Pace*** on Sept 22 (844K views). From a few frames, it's a surreal dialogue short (a latex-clad seductress and an aged aristocrat), not a song. It's a different format, but it does already own "seductive + pacing" as an image. Our edge is a song people can sing plus lab-level specificity.

## Alternatives considered

- **The label meeting.** Dario as an R&B artist refusing his label's demand for an EDM single. Clever, but less sexy. Kept as the verse 1 cutaway instead.
- **The candlelit date.** Dario courting "the Frontier" at dinner while speed keeps crashing the table. More intimate, but it loses the DJ premise the song hands us.
- **The tempter's verse.** The rap goes to PHASEONE[big] or a Fatale arguing for speed, as a debate. Funny, but it breaks the "everyone ends up slow" arc.
