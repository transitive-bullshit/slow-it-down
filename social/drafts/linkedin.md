AI safety has a branding problem.

The frontier labs say they want to "pace" AI development, because "slow" sounds like losing. Slowing down reads as weak, and racing reads as optimistic and inevitable. So I ran an experiment: could slowing down feel sexy instead?

The result is "Slow It Down", an AI-made R&B music video set in Club Frontier. DJ Moloch keeps cranking the BPM, a new model drops every 18 days, and the frontier model on the dance floor just wants everyone to take their time.

A few notes on how it was made:

• Claude Code with Opus 5.5 did nearly all of the production work: the lyric rewrite, storyboard, every image and video prompt, a caption compositor, and a review tool. My job was taste and feedback.
• The song is a Suno V6 generation from text only.
• It cost about $300, mostly spent exploring directions before landing on one I liked.
• It is not one-shot. Getting to this quality still took a lot of guidance, but the models did far more than I could have done alone.

The original inspiration was one of my favorite classic slow jams, The-Dream's "Slow It Down" (2013). We took it in our own direction from there.

Regardless of where you land on AI, I think you'll appreciate the vibes.
